# Audio direction

Status: TBD

Prompts only. Audio assets need provenance (release check A10); music with lyrics or spoken audio is
not allowed in store videos (release item O08).

## Mood
> Prompt: What should the game sound like, in three words?

## Music
> Prompt: When music plays, how it changes with state, and its source and licence.

## Sound effects
> Prompt: Feedback sounds for the core verbs, the UI and the world; variation and priority rules.

## Mix and buses
> Prompt: Buses (master, music, sfx, ui, ambience, dialogue, world, matching the settings/1 volumes) and default levels.

## Listener
> Prompt: Where the listener sits (brief `engine.default_listener_location`) and how 3D sound falls off.

## Accessibility
> Prompt: Subtitles or captions for important sounds; nothing important is conveyed by sound alone.
